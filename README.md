# BioSignal_Expertise - Scientific Consulting in Physiological Data Analysis

I provide scientific consulting and data analysis services for **physiological and biomedical research**, with a focus on **signal processing, statistical modeling, and multimodal physiological data**.

With a **PhD in Neuroscience**, postdoctoral and hospital-university research experience, I develop reproducible analysis pipelines in **Python and R** for experimental and clinical research.

## Areas of expertise

* **EEG** — preprocessing, time-frequency analysis, feature extraction
* **Heart Rate Variability (HRV)** — time- and frequency-domain analysis
* **Respiratory signals** — respiratory dynamics and variability
* **Cardiorespiratory coupling** — RSA and physiological interactions
* **Statistical modeling** — GLM, GLMM, and models adapted to experimental data
* **Multimodal physiological analysis** — integration of EEG, cardiac and respiratory signals
* **Reproducible scientific workflows** — documented and reusable Python/R pipelines
